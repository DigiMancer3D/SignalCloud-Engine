# Community conduct

SignalCloud contributors are expected to communicate respectfully, discuss the work rather than attack people, avoid harassment or discrimination, and protect private or security-sensitive information.

Project maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions and participation that create an unsafe or hostile environment, expose private data, or undermine the project's safety boundaries. Good-faith technical disagreement is welcome when it remains focused on evidence, reproducible behavior, and the shared project goals.
